02 / Extend

One specification. One Foundation. Optional capabilities.

Open Brand Definition is an open specification for governed brand truth and AI creation. The Foundation is always required. It defines Brand Elements, knowledge states, rules, value contracts, scope, provenance and integrity. Everything beyond it is declared, optional and separately testable.

Foundation conformance does not depend on one vendor, one model or one database. Vendor extensions stay optional and namespaced. Breaking semantics require a new major version. The specification is CC BY 4.0 and the implementation layer is Apache 2.0, so an independent implementation needs no permission and cannot be cut off. Section 11 has the detail.

07 / Licensing

Two standard licences. Nothing to ask for.

OBDS is released under CC BY 4.0 and the Apache License 2.0. Neither licence text is modified, both are published here, and commercial implementation needs no permission from anyone.

01
Specification and documentation CC BY 4.0
Covers

The normative specification, the architecture, changelog, migration and quickstart documents, the governance and contribution documents, and this website.

You may

Share and adapt it for any purpose, commercial included, with credit, a link to the licence and a note on what you changed.

02
Implementation layer Apache License 2.0
Covers

The 35 existing public contracts plus optional Task Facts, the release metadata, the reference implementation, the conformance suite and the machine-readable examples.

You may

Use, modify, distribute and sublicense it, including inside closed-source and commercial products, with a patent grant included.

03
Commercial implementation Permitted
Covers

Paid client delivery, agency and consulting work, production use, SaaS and managed services, commercial products and integrations.

You may

All of it, with no separate agreement, no evaluation period and no distinction between commercial and non-commercial users.

04
Official marks Separately governed
Covers

The project name as your product name, the logo, claims of official status, and any future certification claim. No registration is claimed and no certification programme is live.

Always allowed

Truthful compatibility statements. "Implements OBDS 1.0" and a package called obds-parser need no permission. See TRADEMARKS.md.

Neither licence grants trademark rights, and that is deliberate rather than a loophole. CC BY 4.0 section 2(b)(2) and Apache 2.0 section 6 both exclude them. The line is drawn around who publishes the standard, not around who implements it.

Build it, ship it, sell it. No one has to say yes first.
