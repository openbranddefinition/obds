## 1. Purpose

OBDS technical conformance and OBDS certification are different.

Technical conformance means an implementation satisfies the applicable machine-checkable requirements of a named OBDS version.

Certification is a separate trust programme in which Kill The Dragon GmbH verifies a defined implementation, product or service and may grant limited programme-mark rights for a stated term.

Commercial use of protected OBDS Materials remains governed by `LICENSE.md` and applicable commercial licence agreements.

## 2. Tiers

### 2.1 Self-assessed conformance

Self-assessment is not certification.

Any person may run the unmodified official OBDS conformance suite against an implementation under the carve-out in `LICENSE.md` and may publish accurate results.

Permitted wording:

"conforms to OBDS x.y, self-assessed"

Self-assessment:

- does not grant the OBDS Certified badge;
- does not imply verification by Kill The Dragon GmbH;
- does not grant Commercial Use rights beyond the express carve-outs in `LICENSE.md`;
- does not grant additional trademark rights.

### 2.2 OBDS Certified

**OBDS Certified** is planned as a paid programme for products, tools and services.

The intended requirements are:

- conformance against a named OBDS version;
- verification of machine-generated conformance results;
- identification of the product and version;
- an active commercial licence where the relevant commercial use of protected OBDS Materials requires one;
- compliance with applicable programme and trademark terms.

The programme may grant, for its active term:

- use of the **OBDS Certified** word mark or badge for the covered product;
- a verified directory listing;
- a certificate identifying the product, version and OBDS version.

### 2.3 OBDS Certified Partner

**OBDS Certified Partner** is planned as a paid programme for agencies, consultancies and professional-service organisations delivering OBDS-related work.

The intended requirements are:

- an active commercial licence covering the relevant OBDS use, where that use requires one;
- demonstrated OBDS delivery capability;
- a partner assessment;
- compliance with programme and trademark terms.

The programme may grant:

- use of the **OBDS Certified Partner** badge during the active term;
- a verified partner-directory listing;
- service categories and territories;
- eligibility for referrals or co-marketing where offered.

## 3. Conformance basis

Conformance is evaluated against a named OBDS version and its published conformance requirements.

The conformance suite is evidence of technical conformance. It is not by itself a licence, certification or endorsement.

Where the normative specification and a conformance test conflict, the normative specification governs and the test defect must be reported and corrected.

## 4. Planned programme process

The intended process is:

1. **Apply** with organisation, product or service, and target OBDS version.
2. **Confirm licensing status** for any commercial use of protected OBDS Materials.
3. **Run** the applicable conformance suite.
4. **Submit** the machine-generated conformance result.
5. **Verify** the submitted result and perform defined spot checks.
6. **Remediate** material findings where allowed by the programme terms.
7. **Grant** programme status and limited badge rights if all requirements are met.

The binding agreement will define service levels and turnaround times.

## 5. Fees

Self-assessed conformance is intended to remain free.

Paid programme tiers may include:

- an annual fee per covered product;
- an annual Certified Partner fee per organisation;
- re-verification fees where additional review is required.

Exact fees will be published or agreed before the programme becomes active.

## 6. Term and renewal

The intended programme term is twelve (12) months.

Status is tied to:

- the named implementation, product or service;
- the named product version or defined version range;
- the named OBDS version.

Material implementation changes may require re-verification.

A new major OBDS version may require new verification.

## 7. Suspension and revocation

Programme status may be suspended or revoked where:

- the covered implementation materially ceases to conform;
- a programme claim becomes misleading;
- required commercial licensing expires or terminates;
- badge use breaches trademark or programme terms;
- fees remain unpaid.

Binding terms will define notice, cure periods and appeal rights.

On expiry, suspension or revocation, applicable badges and programme claims must cease as required by the agreement.

## 8. Relationship to licensing

Programme status and commercial licensing are separate.

A programme grant:

- does not transfer ownership of OBDS;
- does not automatically grant Commercial Use rights in protected OBDS Materials;
- does not grant rights beyond the specific programme marks and scope stated in the agreement.

Where the covered use is commercial and relies on protected OBDS Materials beyond the carve-outs in `LICENSE.md`, an active commercial licence is required unless the applicable agreement expressly states otherwise.

## 9. Relationship to trademarks

Programme-mark and badge use is a limited trademark permission.

It is governed by:

- `TRADEMARKS.md`;
- the programme agreement;
- any published badge usage rules.

The mark permission ends when the programme right ends.

## 10. Governance and appeals

The planned programme will publish a process for:

- conformance disputes;
- remediation;
- suspension;
- revocation;
- appeals;
- material changes to programme criteria.

An appeal should be reviewed by a person who was not the sole decision-maker in the original verification.
