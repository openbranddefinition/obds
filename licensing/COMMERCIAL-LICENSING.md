## 1. Purpose

The OBDS Free Use Licence permits defined personal, educational, research, testing, evaluation and non-production uses.

It also contains limited commercial-purpose carve-outs for unmodified machine-readable OBDS schemas and use of the official conformance suite.

Commercial exercise of rights in other protected OBDS Materials requires a separate written commercial licence agreement with Kill The Dragon GmbH where applicable law reserves those acts to the rights holder.

This document explains when that licence is normally required and what a commercial agreement may cover.

## 2. Commercial licensing normally applies

A commercial licence is normally required where protected OBDS Materials other than the Section 5 carve-outs in `LICENSE.md` are reproduced, adapted, embedded, distributed, made publicly available or otherwise exercised in a manner reserved to the rights holder within activities such as:

- paid client projects;
- agency services;
- consulting or professional services using protected OBDS Materials;
- paid OBDS training or workshops reproducing protected OBDS Materials;
- internal commercial Production Use;
- SaaS or managed services incorporating protected OBDS Materials;
- commercial software products incorporating protected OBDS Materials;
- paid OBDS-based workflows or deliverables containing protected OBDS Materials or adaptations;
- commercial redistribution or resale of protected OBDS Materials or adaptations.

Reading the public specification, independently implementing its ideas, methods, functionality or formats, using the unmodified schemas under the carve-out in `LICENSE.md`, or running the official conformance suite as permitted by `LICENSE.md` does not by itself create a commercial licence requirement.

## 3. What this policy does not claim

This policy does not claim that Kill The Dragon GmbH owns general ideas, principles, methods, functionality or concepts underlying machine-readable brand governance.

It does not purport to prevent an independently developed system that does not copy, adapt, distribute or otherwise exercise restricted rights in protected OBDS Materials beyond rights already granted and does not misuse protected marks.

## 4. Typical commercial licence scopes

A commercial licence agreement may grant one or more of the following rights:

- internal production use of protected OBDS Materials;
- agency or consulting use;
- customer project delivery;
- commercial software implementation using protected OBDS Materials;
- SaaS or managed service use;
- commercial integrations;
- redistribution of specified OBDS Materials or approved adaptations;
- use by named affiliates, customers or delivery partners.

The commercial licence agreement defines the exact scope, entities, term, fees and restrictions.

## 5. Commercial licence does not automatically include

Unless expressly granted in the signed agreement, a commercial licence does not include:

- ownership of OBDS;
- ownership of the specification, schemas, tests or reference implementations;
- unrestricted sublicensing rights;
- the right to publish a modified specification as an official OBDS release;
- certification status;
- endorsement by Kill The Dragon GmbH;
- rights to the OBDS logo or certification badges;
- the right to use OBDS or Open Brand Definition as a product, service or company name.

## 6. Compatibility and conformance claims

A person may make truthful descriptive statements such as:

- "uses OBDS";
- "implements OBDS 1.0";
- "supports OBDS 1.0";
- "compatible with OBDS 1.0";

where those statements are accurate and otherwise comply with `TRADEMARKS.md`.

Such statements do not imply certification or endorsement.

The official conformance suite may be run under the carve-out in `LICENSE.md`, including against a commercial implementation.

Use of **OBDS Certified**, **OBDS Certified Partner**, associated badges or equivalent claims requires separate certification and trademark permission.

## 7. Relationship to certification

Commercial licensing and certification are separate.

A commercial licence grants the commercial rights stated in the licence agreement.

Certification, where available, verifies specified conformance and may grant limited badge or mark rights.

A commercial licence does not automatically confer certification.

Certification does not automatically grant commercial rights in protected OBDS Materials.
